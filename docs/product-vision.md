# PRODUCT VISION

## Vision

ASCEND is an Agentic AI Productivity Coach.

Unlike traditional reminder apps, ASCEND understands user progress, stores long-term memory, plans future actions, and adapts daily routines.

---

## Mission

Help users become disciplined through intelligent planning instead of repetitive reminders.
